# `apps/notifications`

What this app holds and how its parts fit. Loaded when you work in this directory; the
rules that hold everywhere are in the root `CLAUDE.md`. Keep this file current when the
app changes.

## Layout

- `EmailOutbox`
- `EmailAttempt`
- `backends` (outbox mailer)
- `outbox` (queue helpers)
- `crypto` (sealing)
- `eligibility` (recipient and security rules)
- `dispatch` (`DispatchSource` for orders, incl. `record_attempt`)
- `services/` (a package: `claims` the worker's transactions as `system:worker` (claim, outcome, abandoned attempts, housekeeping, the quarantine); `own` (M17.2) the person's own levels, `set_own_channels` (a switch per channel; `set_email_opt_out` is its email case) and `set_own_notices` (an own choice per notice and channel, `None` = follow the administrators), on the principal's own rows only (no user id; `catalog.view`, session MFA when privileged; idempotent; audited `notification.own_changed` with before and after, branchless; only what differs is written); `preferences` M15.2's `set_notice_default` (the setting for everyone per channel, installation-wide, `org.manage`) and `set_person_notices` (one person's always/never keyed `(notice, channel)`, the whole table in one transaction and one event; a staff account refused, an unknown person 404; idempotent; audited `notification.default_changed` / `notification.person_changed` with before and after; nobody is told); M15.3: `selectors.notice_states`/`person_notices`/`own_notices` read them for the screens (`resolution.decisions` says which level decided), and the screens are `org:notifications` (`/org/notifications/`, Administration › Notifications, notice × channel, one dialog per notice), `org:notification_default` and `org:person_notifications` (`people/<id>/notifications/<channel>/`: the person's page section and one dialog per channel, `apps/organizations/views/notification_screens.py`); M17.4: *My notifications* `notifications:settings` (`/account/notifications/`: 1. where, `save_channels`; 2. what, `save_choices/<channel>/`; inline dialogs `dialogs.py`, forms `forms.py` that write only what differs from the hidden `seen_*` values), the header's `{% bell %}` (`templatetags/notice_tags.py`) and `inapp:` under `/notifications/` (`inbox_views`: the list paged by `apps.core.listing`, the `bell/` fragment GET-only and `@session_passive` polled every 60 s, POST-only `<id>/open/` and `read-all/`))
- `housekeeping` (task registry; M17.3's `in_app_notifications`, registered by the app's `ready`: Q98's purge in batches of `PURGE_BATCH` within `PURGE_BUDGET` seconds, a *batched* task (`register_batched_task`: each batch its own authorized transaction under the worker), each DELETE stating its rule again on the row, `IN_APP_READ_RETENTION`/`IN_APP_UNREAD_RETENTION` days and `IN_APP_MAX_PER_PERSON` from `config/env.py` helpers, a log line with counts per rule, never who)
- `worker` (loop, SMTP dialogue, deadline, heartbeat; `manage.py worker`), M7.1: `notices` (subscribers of `request.submitted`, `request.reviewer_assigned` (M7b.2: the assignee), `request.approved|rejected` and `handover.posted` queue one notification per request inside the publisher's transaction (`request.edited`, M7b.3: one per batch of edits, held back `EDIT_BATCH` through `outbox.waiting`/`defer`): reviewers via `recipients.holders`, the requester via `recipients.user_with`; templates in `templates/notifications/email/`, rendered in `LANGUAGE_CODE`)
- `NotificationPreference` (business-email opt-out, honoured at queueing and at the claim; `services.set_email_opt_out`, `selectors`, `views`/`urls` under `/account/notifications/`), M15.1: `kinds` (the registry of business notices: `Notice` (six stable keys), `NOTICES` (label, who receives it, when, capability, screen order), `Channel` (`EMAIL` and, since M17, `IN_APP`; `CHANNELS` lists each with its default and whether it is opt-in; the Telegram bot's later), `Mode` `ALWAYS`/`NEVER`)
- M17.1: `NoticeChoice` (the person's own choice per notice and channel, on or off; no row = follow the administrators) and `ChannelSwitch` (the person's switch of a channel other than email, a CHECK keeps email out; email's stays `NotificationPreference.email_opt_out`), read-only admin
- `NoticeDefault` (one per notice and channel, no row = on) and `NoticeSetting` (one per person, notice and channel, no row = follow the default), read-only admin
- `resolution` (`wanted`/`filter_wanted`, one query, per channel since M17: the person's switch for the channel, then their own `NoticeChoice`, then the administrator's setting for them, then the default (`decisions` says which level decided); `queue_notification(notice=…)` keeps only who wants it and records the notice on `EmailOutbox.notice` (a CHECK keeps it to `NOTIFICATION`, the frozen-content trigger covers it), `eligible_recipients` applies it again at the claim; a row without a notice follows the opt-out only), M7c.1: `selectors.delivery_status(ctx, object_type, object_ids)` (the latest security mail of each `source_ref` about an invitation or email change, keyed `(id, source_ref)`, one query: state, time, `retrying`, and `delayed` after 10 minutes queued or sending; never body, token, `last_error` or recipient) rendered by `templates/components/delivery_badge.html`
- M17.3: `InAppNotification` (no text: the notice, the request's type and id, `notified_at` by the database clock, `read_at`; indexes for the bell, the list and the purge; one unread row per person and request for a grouped notice)
- `in_app` (`notify`, called by every `notices` subscriber for the email's recipients and kept by `resolution` on `IN_APP`, in the publisher's transaction; a reviewer's edits refresh the unread row; `visible(ctx)`: the person's own rows whose request they still see with the notice's capability, decided in SQL)
- `kinds.NoticeSpec.page` (the request page a notice opens, email link included)
- `selectors.unread_count` (*99+*) and `in_app_notifications` (newest first, with the request's number)
- `services.open_notification`/`mark_all_read` (`services/reading.py`: own rows only through `visible`, anything else 404, idempotent, not audited))

## Business notices: who receives what (ADR-0031, ADR-0032)

Six keys of `kinds.Notice`, each queued with `queue_notification(notice=…)`; who receives one on a channel (`kinds.CHANNELS`: email, in the application; M17, Q93) is decided by the person's switch for the channel first (email's is *no business email*, the others `ChannelSwitch`), then their own `NoticeChoice` (wins over the administrators in both directions), then the administrator's `NoticeSetting` for them, then `NoticeDefault` (no row = the channel's default, on), applied at queueing and again at the claim on email (`resolution`) and in the application at every display (`in_app.visible`: own rows whose request the person still sees with the notice's capability, decided in SQL; anything else 404); the administrators' levels set only by `org.manage` through `set_notice_default` / `set_notice_channels` / `set_person_notices` (a person out of scope 404, a staff account refused, audited, nobody told), the person's only by the person (`set_own_channels` / `set_own_notices`, no user id parameter, audited `notification.own_changed`); in-app rows store no text, reading them is not audited, the bell's poll is `@session_passive`; security mail and supplier dispatches carry no notice and are never filtered by a setting.
