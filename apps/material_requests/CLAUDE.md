# `apps/material_requests`

What this app holds and how its parts fit. Loaded when you work in this directory; the
rules that hold everywhere are in the root `CLAUDE.md`. Keep this file current when the
app changes.

## Layout

- `MaterialRequest` (M7b.2: `assigned_reviewer`/`assigned_at`/`assigned_by`, all or none)
- `MaterialRequestLine` (C9 and C3 composite FKs, scope `request_q`), M7b.3's history (`MaterialRequestBaseline` + `MaterialRequestBaselineLine`, the as-submitted copy taken at every submit, C9 key; `MaterialRequestLineRevision`, one per reviewer edit; append-only by trigger, a line named by id without a foreign key, scoped through the request)
- `revisions` (`values` of a line as a revision stores them, `version` = submits + edits, the `seen_version` a page sends back)
- `reviewers` (M7b.2: who may be assigned a request, `covers`/`candidates`/`assignee`: `request.review` through a GA/MM/ENG membership covering the procurement branch; a stored assignee who no longer qualifies reads as unassigned)
- `transitions` (`ALLOWED_TRANSITIONS`)
- `ledger` (derived A/R/P/Q/D quantities from registered `FlowSource`s: purchasing's R/P/Q/allocated (M5.1), handovers' D (M6.1), live/open predicates, *to purchase*, auto close/reopen eligibility, the deactivation provider)
- `services/` (a package whose `__init__` re-exports the public services; `common`: shared rules, locks, authorization; `requester`: drafts, lines, submit with number, procurement snapshot and optional `reviewer_id`, withdraw, cancel; `assignment`: `assign_reviewer` (any reviewer in scope, `SUBMITTED`/`APPROVED`, note required; the actor's and assignee's `User` rows `FOR SHARE` at L0 before `authorize_in_txn`); `review`:  `decide_lines`/`decide_line`/`approve_all` with finalisation and the self-decision rule, `return_to_draft`, `reduce_approved_qty` (the decisions take an optional `seen_version`); `editing` (M7b.3, `request.edit`): `edit_line`, `add_line_as_reviewer`, `remove_line_as_reviewer` with a `LineEdit`, in `SUBMITTED` (clears the decision; the editor may decide in the same step) and `APPROVED` (approved with it, floors of §4.5, in place only with no live allocation and nothing received or delivered, otherwise `REPLACE`: close short + a new approved line), each a revision against the latest copy; sourcing: `set_sourcing`, `resolve_non_catalog_line`, `change_schedule`, `close_line_short`/`reopen_line`, `manual_close`/`reopen`, automatic close; the ledger-input services lock the request's allocation component through `commitments` (registered by purchasing) before any line, trim the lines they close and reconcile after the automatic close)
- `selectors/` (a package: `requester`, `reviewer`, `request_history`, `project_overview`; M7b.2 adds `eligible_reviewers`, `assignee`, `assigned_to` ("Assigned to me"), `can_assign`; M7b.3 `line_history` (each line with its copy and the edits since), `version`, `can_edit_lines`; M7b.4 `inbox(..., assigned_to_me=)`, `assigned_ids` and `assigned_to(..., statuses=)`)/`forms`/`views/` (`requester`, `reviewer`, `closing` (the closures and the schedule), `editing`, `trace`, `common`)/`urls` (the requester's screens under `/requests/`: project → materials → review → submit → status, "My requests"; `submit` also takes `priority`/`needed_by`/`notes`; the reviewer's under the same prefix: `inbox/` (approval inbox with priority, needed-by and project filters), `<id>/examine/` (review page: per-line decisions, approve all (from the head of the page in a dialog, M14.2, and from the bottom bar), return for changes, the material manager's sourcing, reduce, schedule, close/reopen, link-to-catalog dialog and the audit history; every action is its own POST-only URL that flashes the outcome and redirects back to the page at the card of the line it concerns (`#line-<n>`, `_examine_url(found, line_no)`; a decision goes to the next pending line, `selectors.next_pending`, else to the top); M7b.4: the assigned reviewer and a reassign dialog (`<id>/assign/`), the reviewer's edit tools per line (`lines/<id>/edit/` dialog with the material searched by GET and chosen with `?material=`, saved by `lines/<id>/apply-edit/`, "save and approve" in a submitted request; `<id>/lines/new/` → `<id>/lines/apply-add/`; `lines/<id>/remove-reviewed/`; `views/editing.py`), every decision, approve-all and edit form carrying the page's `seen_version` as a hidden field, the line against its as-submitted copy and its edits (`line_edits.html`), and the inbox's "Assigned to me" filter), the submit step's optional reviewer picker (choices from `eligible_reviewers`), and the requester's status page with the edited lines (`line_changes.html`), `data-testid` selectors for the e2e layer)
- the read-only traceability screens (M6.5, `request.view`): `<id>/ledger/` (every ledger quantity per line, closure provenance, and for who follows the request up the allocation trims from `purchasing.selectors.request_trims`, never the order or supplier) and `projects/<id>/materials/` (`selectors.ProjectMaterials`: open lines of the visible requests summed per material, alphabetical, read from a cursor)
- `templates/material_requests/`
- `commitments` (the `ComponentLocker` seam: lock, trim, reconcile, `allocated_lines`)
